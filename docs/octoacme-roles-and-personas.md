# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/Design Leads

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads shape usable, accessible experiences that address user needs and align with product goals.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and synthesize findings
- Create and maintain interaction flows, prototypes, and visual designs
- Define usability and accessibility considerations for acceptance
- Validate the delivered experience against designs and user needs

### How this role interacts with other roles
- Partner with Product Managers to translate user needs and product goals into experience requirements and priorities
- Collaborate with Developers to assess feasibility, clarify designs, and resolve implementation trade-offs
- Work with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility expectations in test plans and acceptance checks
- Share research findings and design decisions with Project Managers and Stakeholders to support planning and alignment

---

## Data/Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Leads make project outcomes measurable and help teams use trustworthy data to evaluate results.

### Responsibilities
- Turn agreed success metrics into measurement and instrumentation requirements
- Define analytics events and reporting needs, and validate data quality
- Establish baselines and report outcome metrics after delivery
- Identify measurement gaps and communicate limitations in the data

### How this role interacts with other roles
- Partner with Product Managers, who own product success metrics and decisions, to define how outcomes will be measured and interpreted
- Work with Developers to implement instrumentation and resolve data collection issues
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to verify analytics events and reporting as part of feature validation
- Share metric status and findings with Project Managers and Stakeholders for project tracking and follow-up

---

## Security/Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security/Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security and privacy requirements and risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on security and privacy requirements, controls, and applicable reviews
- Identify risks and coordinate assessment of proposed mitigations
- Review security and privacy findings and track them through resolution or explicit risk acceptance
- Support incident escalation through the established security incident process

### How this role interacts with other roles
- Work with Project Managers to record security and privacy risks, owners, mitigations, and escalation status
- Partner with Product Managers and Stakeholders to clarify requirements and communicate material trade-offs
- Collaborate with Developers on secure implementation and with QA/Testing on verification of controls and fixes
- Escalate security incidents through the security incident runbook and notify Security on-call, in coordination with the Project Manager

---

## Operations/Release Leads

### Role Summary
Operations/Release Leads coordinate production readiness and operational handoff so changes can be deployed, monitored, and supported reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, including monitoring, alerting, and support handoff
- Coordinate release readiness, deployment checks, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Verify post-deployment health and ensure known issues are communicated
- Capture operational follow-up actions and route them to accountable owners

### How this role interacts with other roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release milestones, dependencies, readiness status, and escalations
- Work with Product Managers and Stakeholders to align release timing and communicate impact
- Partner with Developers on deployment, observability, and remediation plans, and with QA/Testing on release verification and smoke tests
- Ensure support owners receive the context and procedures needed to respond after launch

---

## Assigning Roles

Assign a clear owner for each responsibility on every project. On smaller projects, one person may cover multiple roles, but ownership and required reviews should remain explicit. Escalations should follow the relevant project, release, or security incident process.
