# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's project management guidance. OctoAcme manages work through an iterative lifecycle: initiate and validate an idea, plan scope and delivery, execute and track progress, release with verification and rollback readiness, and capture lessons through retrospectives and continuous improvement. Teams manage risks and communicate throughout every stage.

## Project lifecycle and processes

1. **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define goals and success measures, identify stakeholders and resources, record initial risks, and agree whether to proceed to planning.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria and estimates. Agree on the Definition of Done, dependencies, milestones, release plan, and QA approach.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver in small increments using a project board, regular team touchpoints, pull request reviews, and progress metrics. Surface blockers early and coordinate dependencies.
4. **Risk management and communication:** Record risks and dependencies with owners, mitigations, and status; review them regularly. Share status, next steps, blockers, and decisions needed with stakeholders, escalating issues through the defined paths.
5. **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans. Deploy and verify in staging and production, then communicate the release.
6. **Retrospectives and continuous improvement:** After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture what went well and what could improve. Assign owners and due dates to a small set of actions, then follow up and assess impact.
